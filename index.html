Pro Keyboard Cleaning & Sanitization Guide

1. Recommended Disinfectant

Use 70% Isopropyl Alcohol (IPA) lightly misted onto a lint-free microfiber cloth. Never pour or spray liquids directly onto keyboard keys or laptop chassis.

2. Dealing with Sticky Keys & Spills

If a switch is sticky, unplug the keyboard immediately. For mechanical keyboards, use a keycap puller to remove the keycap and clean the stem housing with a cotton swab dampened with 99% alcohol.

3. Compressed Air Technique

Hold the compressed air canister upright at a 45-degree angle. Blow in short bursts moving row by row to prevent moisture discharge into switch mechanisms.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does KeyClean Pro require downloading an app or extension?
No! KeyClean Pro is 100% web-based. It runs client-side in Safari, Chrome, Edge, and Firefox without requiring software downloads, admin rights, or browser extensions.
How does Hold-to-Unlock prevent accidental unlocking?
Standard online cleaners unlock with a single inadvertent click. During heavy wiping, a cloth or palm can easily hit the mouse button. Our 2-second hold gauge ensures only intentional unlocks are registered.
Does it work on MacBook Pro and Windows laptops?
Yes! KeyClean Pro includes native layouts for macOS (Command ⌘, Option ⌥) and Windows/PC laptops, plus a complete 100% full-size keyboard with numpad.
What is Simultaneous Pressure (Rollover)?
When wiping multiple keys simultaneously with a cloth, your keyboard registers multiple inputs at once. KeyClean Pro measures multi-key rollover in real-time to benchmark your keyboard's anti-ghosting matrix.
